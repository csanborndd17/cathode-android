# Cathode 0.11.1 — Home grid and synchronized lyrics

## What changed

- Restores the phone Home screen to a stable two-column card grid.
- Removes the misleading layout-resolution/density override and uses Android's detected display metrics.
- Keeps responsive full-width hero and shelf content across phone sizes.
- Replaces red, green, and blue sliders with a draggable full-spectrum color field while retaining theme presets.
- Adds anonymous lyric lookup through LRCLIB, preferring synchronized LRC lyrics and falling back to plain lyrics.
- Turns Lyrics into an artwork-backed Now Playing mode that follows the active timed line and lets the listener tap a timed line to seek.
- Keeps downloaded/imported lyrics locally editable in the existing metadata editor.

## Display-resolution boundary

An ordinary Android application cannot change a device panel's physical output resolution. Cathode now always uses the resolution and density reported by Android and adapts its layout to the available window. Changing system display resolution requires device-level settings or privileged ADB/OEM access and is intentionally outside the app.

## Lyrics and privacy

Automatic lookup sends the edited track title, artist, optional album, and duration to `https://lrclib.net`. It requires no Cathode or LRCLIB account. Lyrics are not fetched until the listener taps **Find lyrics**, and results are not saved until **Save** is tapped.

On-device song transcription is not included: accurate music transcription requires a large speech model, significant processing and battery use, and still needs manual synchronization. Local LRC import and editing remain available.
